Privacy Policy — Pearlstone Technologies

Effective: October 18, 2026 (proposed, subject to approval before publishing)

Pearlstone Technologies LLC (“Pearlstone,” “we,” “us”) operates the corporate information website at pearlstonetechnologies.com. This policy concerns this website only; it is not the privacy policy for the unreleased EnvelopeCircle mobile app.

Information we collect

Information you send to us. If you email us, we receive your email address and any information in your message. The corporate website currently has no account creation, payment collection or web-based contact form.

Technical information. Our hosting and security services may process request information such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps, approximate location inferred from network information and security events to serve and protect the website.

Analytics. We have not added a dedicated visitor-analytics tracker or advertising pixel to this corporate website. If this changes, we will update this policy.

Cookies, similar technologies and choices

We do not intentionally deploy analytics, advertising or retargeting cookies from this corporate website. Our hosting or security infrastructure may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms when delivering the site. If we add optional tracking technologies requiring consent, we will provide applicable choice controls before activating them.

Browsers may provide Global Privacy Control (GPC) or “Do Not Track” settings. We do not sell personal information or use cross-context behavioral advertising on this site. You may also use browser privacy controls or tracking protection.

Why we process information

We use information to operate and secure the site, respond to inquiries, and communicate with business and partnership contacts, comply with legal requirements and prevent misuse.

Service providers and disclosures

We use Netlify to host and deliver the corporate website. Service providers process information to supply their services subject to applicable arrangements. We may disclose information to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or as part of a legitimate corporate transaction.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention

Business inquiries are retained as needed to respond and maintain relevant correspondence; hosting and security records are kept for operational and security needs. Retention periods may vary by data category, provider settings and legal requirements; we do not promise a fixed period that has not been established.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information by writing to ikenna@pearlstonetechnologies.com. We will verify and respond as applicable law requires, generally within 30 days where feasible. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights and appeal options.

Security

We use proportionate technical and organizational safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This business-to-business corporate website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly solicit their personal information. Contact us if you believe information about a child has been submitted.

International visitors

We are a United States company. Information may be processed in the United States or wherever our contracted service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

Changes

We will post revisions with an updated effective date. Where required or appropriate for material changes, we will provide additional notice.
