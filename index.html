Owned Product

EnvelopeCircle™

EnvelopeCircle is Pearlstone Technologies LLC's financial coordination product, designed to help people organize budgets, upcoming commitments, goals and shared saving plans with greater clarity.

Buckets
Organize what money is intended for.
Payment Windows
Coordinate upcoming bills and timing.
Circles
Coordinate shared saving commitments and turns.
Shared Planning
Improve visibility around shared goals and household coordination.

Your money never touches EnvelopeCircle. EnvelopeCircle organizes the plan—not participant funds.
